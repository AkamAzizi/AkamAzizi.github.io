About

A quick snapshot of who I am and what I build.

I’m a third-year Applied AI student at Mälardalens University. I enjoy taking ideas from “sketch in a notebook” to something that real people can click, tap, and use.

My current flagship is Adaptive Boss Battle, a mobile learning game that evaluates users on accuracy, confidence, and speed, then adjusts difficulty, mastery, and pacing in real time.

Outside of games, I’ve built an AI Resume Analyzer (FastAPI + NLP), a Micro Challenges habit app in React Native, a Flappy Bird clone, a Snake game, and several algorithm projects (Sudoku solver, Knapsack suite, Mancala AI).

I like roles where I can contribute quickly, own small features end-to-end, and keep learning by shipping and getting feedback.
